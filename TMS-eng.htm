Transport Management System (es2 TMS)

Vehicle fleet is an expensive investment and its performance has a direct impact to customers and their business, hence it demands a good support to ensure cost effectiveness and customer satisfaction. EL provides our clients with the ability to effectively manage the performance of their own fleet through a fully integrated solution. The major functions includes the followings:

  • Fleet scheduling
  • Delivery orders scheduling  
  • Route planning
  • Truck loading management
  • Drivers allocation
  • Management reports
