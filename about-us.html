About Us

eLogistics Hong Kong Limited ('EL) is committed in developing and implementing computer applications for their clients to effectively manage all logistics activities throughout the entire supply chain for efficiency and better customer satisfaction.

EL has a strong team with multi-talent; their team members have extensive exposure in all areas relating to the design and implementation of large scale logistics projects including the storage and distribution of pharmaceutical items, OTC items for supermarkets, 3PL operations, cold storage operations, power stations, etc. The projects that developed were in various parts of the world, that includes Mainland China , Hong Kong , Taiwan , Singapore , Thailand , UK , etc.

In order to better serve their clients, EL has offices both in Hong Kong as well as in Guangzhou , PRC.
