Work Order System (ES2 WOS)

In the highly competitive business environment as nowadays, traditional warehousing operations can hardly justify the huge investment for a warehouse, hence most of the 3PL would upgrade their warehouse services by providing value-added services on top to make the offer more attractive.  ES2 WOS is the application helping 3PLs to organize these activities in the smoothest and most cost effective manner.  The major functions are:
  • Work order creation and maintenance
  • Stock bonding for the carrying out of value-added activities
  • Virtual item creation and management
  • Packing management
  • Management reporting
