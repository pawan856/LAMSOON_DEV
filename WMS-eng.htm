Warehouse Management System (es2 WMS)

Es2 WMS is a comprehensive and sophisticated warehouse management application. It’s feature covers not just all necessary functions for warehouse operations in accordance with the contemporary ‘Best Practices’, but it also supports various value-added activities that enable users, especially 3PL practitioners, to provide more comprehensive services to their clients for higher customer satisfaction. Through Internet connections, ES2 WMS allows managers and clients to oversee both warehouse operations and updated stockholdings of goods in multi-warehouses at multi-locations for timely management decisions.  For small to medium 3PL operators, ES2 WMS will generate invoices to their clients in accordance with the services provided on a regular basis in multi-currency.  Major functions of ES2 WMS are: 

  • Goods in/out management
  • Stock location management
  • Wave picking
  • Stock consolidation
  • Value-added activities
  • Virtual stock management
  • Supports bar codes and RFID operations
  • Management reporting
