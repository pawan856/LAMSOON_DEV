Order Tracking System (ES2 OTS)

Consequent to the globalization of commercial activities, sourcing and merchandizing is no longer localized to any particular location but is becoming more on a ‘multi-locations, multi-sources’ basis to maximize benefits and savings. A new challenge however, is how to effectively manage the vast volume of purchase orders from all over the world, and subsequently the logistics arrangements, customs clearance to ensure key dates along the supply chain are met for customer satisfaction. Pin-pointing this, ES2’s web based Order Tracking System (ES2 OTS) is designed to provide a complete solution and its major functions are:

  • Key dates monitoring/reminding

  • Order placement

  • Order acknowledgement

  • Order track and trace

  • Status enquiry

  • Inventory monitoring
